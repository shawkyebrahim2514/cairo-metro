# Non-metro transit research (23 Nov 2026)

Sources are news reports quoting the Ministry of Transport (Ahram, Youm7, Masrawy, Al-Masry Al-Youm, Cairo360); no official operator page was reached. No station coordinates were found, and OSM coverage is unverified. All items are **unverified** until checked against an official source. Do not copy data from other apps.

## Ring Road BRT (الأتوبيس الترددي)

- Phase 1: 14 stations (Alexandria Agricultural Rd to Police Academy). Phase 2 opened 26 Sep 2026 (Mushir Tantawi to Sun Capital); 30 stations, 75 km in total. Whether Mokattam, Zahraa Maadi, Autostrad and Mansouriya are running is unclear (Cairo360 and Ahram conflict).
- Fares by stations travelled: 4 → 5, 10 → 10, 14 → 15, 18 → 20, 22 → 25, 26 → 30, 30 → 35 EGP.
- Hours 05:00 to 01:00; headway 3 min (Phase 1 figure).
- Interchanges: L1 at El-Zahraa and El-Marg; L3 at Adly Mansour (and Imbaba, which is not in the published lists); LRT at Adly Mansour; East Nile Monorail at Mushir Tantawi.
- Stations 1–14: Alexandria Agricultural Rd, Col. Ahmed Abdel Rahim, Shubra–Banha, Bahtim, Mostorod, Khusus, Marg, Qalag, Zakat Foundation, Lt. Gen. Ibrahim Orabi, Salam, Adly Mansour, Suez Road, Police Academy.
- Stations 15–30 (order inferred): Mushir Tantawi, Golf, Ain Sokhna Rd, Maadi, Zahraa Maadi, Mokattam, Autostrad, Algeria Axis, Imamain, Zahraa, Bahr Aazam, Omraniya, Talbiya, Maryouteya, Mansouriya, Sun Capital.

## East Nile Monorail (مونوريل شرق النيل)

- Opened ~6 May 2026; 22 stations, 56.5 km, about 70 min end to end.
- Fares (zones of 5/10/15/full): 20 / 40 / 55 / 80 EGP; seniors and disabled half price. Monthly passes 600 / 1,200 / 1,650 / 2,400.
- Hours and headway conflict: May 06:00 to 18:00 every 6–8 min; Sep 06:00 to 19:50 every 12 min (more recent).
- Interchanges: L3 at Cairo Stadium; LRT at Arts & Culture City; BRT at Mushir Tantawi.
- Stations: Cairo Stadium, Hesham Barakat, Al-Azhar University, 7th District, Field Marshal Ahmed Ismail, Jehan Sadat, Field Marshal Tantawi, "وان ناينتي" (English name unverified), Air Force Hospital, Narges, Investors, Lotus, Golden Square, Beit El-Watan, Al-Fattah Al-Alim Mosque, R1, R2, Business District, Arts & Culture City, Government District, Misr Mosque, Justice City.

## LRT and West Nile Monorail

- LRT (Adly Mansour to 10th of Ramadan) is operating. Fares are 10 (up to 3 stations), 15 (up to 7) and 20 EGP; monthly passes 300 / 500 / 600. Full station list and hours not captured.
- West Nile Monorail (6th of October): not in service; trial of 8 of 13 stations was planned for Oct 2026. Not ready.

## Recommendation

1. East Nile Monorail and BRT Phase 1 first, as optional layers; BRT Phase 2 behind a per-station "open" flag.
2. Get coordinates from OSM or an official map before shipping; survey and add to OSM if missing.
3. Resolve open questions: which BRT stations are running, BRT stations 17–30 order, monorail hours and headway, the "One Nineteen" name, Imbaba interchange, ticket medium, travel times.
