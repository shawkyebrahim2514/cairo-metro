Idea: when a trip is long, the picture is cut at a transfer or the middle station into two zoomed panels. Each panel shows only its part, so the route is big and every name has room. The cut station is repeated at the bottom of panel 1 and the top of panel 2.

How it would work

  • New image option Split long trips (off by default), shown only for trips of 20+ stations.
  • Cut at the transfer if there is one, otherwise at the middle station; two panels stacked in the portrait or story format (or two separate images to share).
  • Each panel keeps its own north arrow if the map is turned, and its own station names.
  • Cost: medium. It needs a second projection and clipping in the renderer, plus a list column per panel.
